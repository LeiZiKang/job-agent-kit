05 / Friday review

Learn from the week.
Adjust the next one.

Ask what worked, what is missing, and what to change. At most three adjustments, with evidence and a date to check again.

Terminal — weekly review

$ python3 bin/jt.py funnel --by source

Demo data · exact output from examples/tracker.json

Source            Applied  Replied  Reply rate  Interviews  Rejected
(unrecorded)            8        0          0%           0         1
Total                   8        0          0%           0         1

Claude › Three adjustments for the next review:

  1. Record known sources first. All 8 records are unrecorded; there is no basis for ranking channels yet.
  2. Repair missing milestones from evidence. Northwind is interviewing, but no interview date is recorded. Don’t read the zero as “no interviews”.
  3. Before shifting effort, compare like-aged cohorts with --since and inspect --by platform. Reassess next Friday; this demo sample is small and incomplete.

This is the current tracker’s exact calculation, not an outcome claim: 8 records include one draft; zero replies and interviews means no milestone fields are recorded. Source is not platform.
