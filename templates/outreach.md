# Pre-approved outward actions ("do, then report the same day")

All boxes start unticked. Copy this to local `outreach.md`; the user approves each scope and
its filled-in text before it becomes permission. Placeholders are not permission. Facts and
resume selection come from `profile.md`. Report target, exact text, platform, attachment,
time and observed confirmation the same day. Never expand a checked box to adjacent actions.
Opening unread chats still needs authorization. No template authorizes credentials or settings.

## T1 — Apply via platform
- [ ] Approved for {{platforms}}, {{screened job scope}}, {{approved greeting}}, {{resume selection}}.

Only lead-reviewed `recommend` jobs qualify. Put `maybe` jobs in the decision pool first.
Run `jt seen` and `jt company` before applying. Inspect delivery evidence for any company match;
one role per company by default. A justified exception needs a recorded reason, not a silent bypass.
Check whether the button automatically sends a greeting, online resume or attachment; those
outward effects must be within the approval. Verify the final submission confirmation.

> Hello, I am interested in {{role}}. {{One relevant, verified fact from profile.md}}.
> I would be glad to learn more about the team and its working arrangement. Thank you.

A customized first sentence may mention one JD-specific fit point only if approved; do not
repeat the background sentence or add skills absent from the profile.

## T1b — Contact an engineering lead directly
- [ ] Approved for {{public hiring posts or named platform scope}} and {{approved text scope}}.

For an engineer, engineering manager or founder who is hiring: one concrete project point and
one approved portfolio link. Do not assemble a list of buzzwords or imply a prior application.

> Hi {{recipient}}, I saw your post about {{role}}. I am {{candidate name}}, {{verified background}}.
> {{One relevant project fact}}. My work: {{approved portfolio link}}.
> Would you be open to discussing what the team is building?

## T2 — First reply to an inbound recruiter
- [ ] Approved for {{inbound message scope}}.

> Hi {{recipient}}, thanks for reaching out about {{role}}. {{Verified one-line background}}.
> I am looking for {{targets from profile.md}}. Could you share the company or client,
> team size and working arrangement? Happy to share my resume if there is a fit.

Do not introduce compensation numbers or availability promises unless separately approved.

## T3 — Send a resume when requested
- [ ] Approved for {{request scope}}, using {{current resume variant from profile.md}}.

> Here is my resume. Thank you, {{recipient}}.

Confirm the attachment name and language. An online profile card is not the same as the file.

## T4 — Follow up once
- [ ] Approved after {{silence interval}} days, via {{channels}}.

Only if there is no rejection and no prior follow-up for this role. A second follow-up needs
fresh approval. Check the live last message first; the user may have replied elsewhere.

> Hi {{recipient}}, following up on {{role}}. I am still interested — {{one verified fit point}}.
> Please let me know if any additional information would help. Thank you.

## T5 — LinkedIn connections
Set {{daily invitation cap}} and {{weekly budget}} with the user. Check the current account's

### T5a — Recruiter invitation note
- [ ] Approved for {{recruiter scope}}.

> Hi {{recipient}}, I am {{candidate name}}, {{verified specialty}}. I applied for {{role}} at
> {{company}} and would be glad to connect.

Use only when the application is confirmed. Otherwise draft a factual alternative for approval.

### T5b — Engineer invitation note
- [ ] Approved for {{team or engineer scope}}.

> Hi {{recipient}}, I work on {{verified specialty}} and am interested in {{team}} at {{company}}.
> I would be glad to connect and learn about your work.

### T5c — Message after an invitation is accepted
- [ ] Approved for one message after acceptance, within {{contact scope}}.

> Thanks for connecting, {{recipient}}! I applied for {{role}} ({{job ID}}).
> If my background looks relevant, would you be open to referring me? Happy to share my resume.
> I would also be interested to hear how the team works.

Check acceptance and application evidence first. If no application exists, use T1b only if its
scope was approved; do not claim an application or automatically send this message.

Confirm the sent item before recording delivery. Follow-up uses the separately approved T4 scope.

## Outside these templates — ask first

Show the specific target, action and final text, then wait for approval before:
- Negotiating compensation, accepting an offer or making contractual commitments.
- Scheduling interviews, promising availability or declining an opportunity.
- Explaining employment relationships, residence, work authorization or reasons for leaving
  beyond the approved factual wording in `profile.md`.
- Sending any message outside a checked template's channel, recipient or wording scope.
- Changing platform profiles, accepting agreements, changing notification settings or exchanging
  contact details beyond an explicitly approved scope.
